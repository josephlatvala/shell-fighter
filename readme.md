# Jam Info

Theme: Shell

Time: until monday


# Initial idea

> this is what we brainstormed, and our more refined idea was some sort of top down shooter where the character is a former fisherman who "comes out of his shell" by finally returning to his favorite fishing spot, which is now overrun by mutant/dangerous/aggressive enemy shellfish & shell-based enemies.  we also talked about the key theme of shotgun/gun shells being involved as your weaponry, and having different types of shells with diff. properties (i.e. heavier enemies need a grenade shell to be killed).  we also thought of a shop idea with enemies dropping shell fragments so that they could either be shot or used as currency in a roguelike fashion
> 
> other ideas:

> -fishing pole/hook as an alt. fire or alternate item to latch on to enemies & pull them towards you with its hook
